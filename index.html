💬 Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Sumair Tools v8.0.

Which versions of Adobe After Effects and operating systems are supported? +
Sumair Tools v8.0 is 100% native ExtendScript & CEP and supports Adobe After Effects CC 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026+ on both macOS (Apple Silicon M1, M2, M3, M4 & Intel) and Windows 10/11. No third-party paid plugins or subscriptions are required.
Is the $3 license really lifetime with no recurring fees or renewals? +
Yes, 100%! As part of our launch for the motion design and video editing community, $3 (discounted from the standard $25 price) grants you a genuine lifetime license key with unlimited personal workstations. All future v8.x updates, bug fixes, and script enhancements are included with zero recurring subscriptions or hidden costs.
What productivity features are included in the Shortcuts & Tools overhaul? +

The Shortcuts & Tools panel has been completely re-architected to eliminate manual timeline friction:

  • Paste Image from Clipboard: Grab any screenshot or copied image from your clipboard (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V), auto-save it as a lossless PNG into your project's imported_images folder, and drop it straight onto your active comp in one click.
  • Comp & Unprecomp: Bundle layers into clean sub-compositions or unwrap/extract nested pre-comps directly back onto the main timeline while preserving layer order, blend modes, and transform keyframes.
  • Split & Trim 3-in-1 Engine: Razor-sharp layer split at CTI, Left Trim (Alt+[), and Right Trim (Alt+]) protected with safe open/close undo grouping.
  • Custom FPS Switcher: Switch comp frame rates between 12, 24, 25, 29.97, 30, and 60 FPS instantly across your active or selected compositions.
  • Batch Sequence with Crossfades: Automatically stagger dozens of layers with custom overlap frame durations and smooth opacity crossfades.
  • Render Queue Quick-Dispatch: Send compositions to AE's Render Queue with 1-click presets for ProRes 422, ProRes 4444 (with alpha transparency), MP4, or PNG sequences.
How does the Media Assets Hub (Video Viewer, Script Board, Storyboard) work? +

The Media Assets tab serves as your personal mission control directly docked inside After Effects:

  • Video Viewer: Paste any reference video file or direct video URL to inspect animations, client cuts, or pacing guides with play, pause, seek, and loop controls without opening external media players.
  • Script Board: Paste voiceover scripts, narration transcripts, or client briefs directly into the panel so you never have to tab out to Notion or Word while editing.
  • Storyboard Picture Slider: Load multiple concept stills, storyboards, animatics, or style-frames and slide back and forth with interactive arrow controls to match your timeline animation 1:1.
How does the Asset Downloader handle YouTube, Instagram, and Pinterest links? +
Powered by an integrated yt-dlp binary and bundled FFmpeg transcoder, simply paste any video URL from YouTube (standard & Shorts), Instagram Reels, or Pinterest. Sumair Tools downloads the highest-quality stream, automatically transcodes it into an AE-friendly H.264 MP4 with AAC audio (preventing the dreaded "QuickTime or compression format not supported" error), and calls app.project.importFile() to drop the file directly into your After Effects Project Bin.
How does the Local AI Background Remover work, and what is the isnet-anime model? +

Sumair Tools features a 100% local, offline AI background removal pipeline powered by Python's rembg library and ONNX runtime—meaning zero cloud API costs, no image upload limits, and complete data privacy. You can toggle models on the fly:

  • isnet-anime (Default): Specifically trained on 2D character art, anime, cel-shaded illustrations, and graphics for crisp, razor-sharp edge cutouts with zero halo artifacts.
  • u2net / isnet-general-use: Ideal for real-world photographs, human portraits, products, and commercial cutouts.

The resulting cutout is saved locally as a transparent 32-bit PNG and automatically imported onto your active composition.

How does the Iconify 150,000+ vector icon library work? +
The Icons module connects directly to Iconify's vector ecosystem covering over 150,000 icons across Material Design (mdi), Lucide (lucide), Phosphor (ph), Boxicons (bx), and Tabler (tabler). Search queries use batch JSON loading with in-memory caching to prevent rate limits, render crisp vector previews, automatically enforce pure white vector fills (#ffffff), and download raw SVGs directly to disk before executing AE's native vector import script.
What is SFX Studio and how does ⚡ AUTO-PLACE TO CUTS work? +
SFX Studio bundles high-impact sound design essentials (Cinematic Whooshes, Risers, Sub Bass Hits, UI Clicks, Pops, and Digital Glitches) ready for 1-click timeline addition. The signature ⚡ AUTO-PLACE TO CUTS engine scans your active edit track or key markers, detects every cut point, and snaps contextual sound effects precisely to each cut across the timeline in under a second—saving you hours of manual audio alignment.
How does Sumair Tools achieve "3 Hours of Work Done in 1 Hour"? +
In traditional After Effects workflows, editors lose up to 70% of their billable hours switching windows: searching stock sites, transcribing speech, removing image backgrounds in external apps, downloading icons one by one, creating nested pre-comps, typing expression math for bounces, and aligning SFX to cuts. Sumair Tools unifies all of these operations into 1-click native ExtendScript automations directly inside your AE workspace, easily tripling your editing speed.
How does the Auto B-Roll 6–12s pre-comp engine work? +
The engine scans your project audio or imported SRT subtitle timestamps, detects natural 6-to-12-second dialogue gaps, automatically generates 2 self-contained compositions containing 1 to 3 synchronized clips from your niche footage folders (Roofing, HVAC, Real Estate, etc.), auto-scales them to fill without letterboxing, applies smooth Ken Burns zoom, and places them onto your timeline with zero black bars.
Does Sumair Tools require an active internet connection to run? +
The vast majority of Sumair Tools—including Master Tools, Shortcuts & Comp engines, Dan Ebberts Physics, 9-Point Smart Anchor, Auto B-Roll, SFX Studio, and Local AI Background Removal (once the ONNX model is cached)—runs 100% offline using your local CPU/GPU. An active internet connection is only needed when fetching online assets via yt-dlp, querying the Iconify vector API, or running Whisper AI cloud speech transcription.
